# Lil-Guy
You play as a lil guy who has to log in to your account and solve the captchas to prove that you're not small. You have to move physically around the laptop to type out letters and use the touchpad. This is a short (hehe) game made in GameMaker Studio 2. I worked on this with a classmate for a school project back in February 2026, where I handled all the planning and programming while they handled the assets. 

The main idea I had for the project is to create a game where you have indirect control over something, in this case a computer. It was about taking an action that most people are familiar with and recontextualize how it's controlled. Everyone I showed it to in class said the idea sounds absolutely unbearable, and that was exactly the response I wanted.

# Controls
WASD - movement up/left/down/right
Spacebar - press key/toggle touchpad cursor mode

# Instructions
After putting in your log in credentials, the player is tasked with doing a series of captchas each with a 30-second limit. This is to prove that you are NOT a tiny creature that is just slowly moving around a laptop.
